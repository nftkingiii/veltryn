# Veltryn demo runbook

Target: 2–3 minutes. Use a clean browser session and the production URL.

1. Open https://veltryn-production.up.railway.app/ and show the landing promise: “The trade can wait. Rehearse it first.”
2. Select “Rehearse a trade”. Point out the read-only boundary: Veltryn researches and models; the user decides.
3. Keep the default NVDA / USDT instrument, Long direction, and thesis endpoint. Mention that the observed mark price, funding, and candles come from Bitget’s public market endpoints when live.
4. Set the adverse move slider to a visible shock level. Click “Review live results”.
5. Explain the chart in order: observed hourly candles, entry divider, calm path, shock-recovery path, and the loss-budget line. Emphasize that both paths can finish at the same endpoint.
6. Show the two result cards and the “What the model used” section. Separate observed Bitget data from deterministic assumptions and historical context.
7. Add one HTTPS research source with a supporting or counter stance. Show the verified excerpt in the evidence area.
8. Save the rehearsal, open Library, reopen the record, and show that its captured snapshot and revision are preserved.
9. Export JSON/CSV or print to PDF. If useful, create a public report and revoke it afterward.

## Claims to use

- “Veltryn is a read-only rehearsal workbench for Bitget stock perpetual decisions.”
- “The live market inputs are public Bitget observations; the paths are deterministic modeled scenarios.”
- “The product makes the path and loss budget visible before a human decides.”

## Claims to avoid

- Do not call the deterministic engine a live AI forecast.
- Do not claim exact liquidation, depth, execution, profitability, or order placement.
- Do not imply that saving an anonymous workspace is a multi-device account.
